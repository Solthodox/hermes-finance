# Source Quality

Use whenever a conclusion depends on web, social, API, analyst, whale-flow, or model-generated evidence.

## Ranking

1. Primary source or official API/event document.
2. Direct data with reproducible query and timestamp.
3. Reputable reporting with named sources.
4. Specialist analysis with transparent method.
5. Social/anonymous/aggregated commentary.
6. Model-generated summaries without citations.

Mark conflicts explicitly. Do not launder weak claims by repeating them across multiple secondary sources. For market-moving facts, prefer timestamped primary evidence and preserve raw captures when practical.
